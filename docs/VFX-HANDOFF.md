## Key decisions and reasoning

- **The founder's two principles are structural, not polish** (plan §2 P1, P2): energy has a fixed origin and propagates as a lit window over a discharge fixed in space; effects on a surface are woven into it with cells that persist. Every form spec in the plan is written against them and against two sim facts the reviewers surfaced: dash/blink records play with the body already at the end point (the sim teleports it in one tick; no travel phase; the stand's body never moves), so every trail is time-driven from the start point; `pull` always points at the caster, so gravity reads as weight and draws the pull only on impact.
- **Not every element has every form** is enforced two ways: the registry's per-element `forms` (deliveries) with an `E1` rule, and the viewer kinds that reach every element regardless (impact, status, charge, wall) — every new module ships `status` and a wall palette pass, the stock silhouette draws the rest.
- **Adding an element is gated**: four places pin five elements (`selfTest`, `checkspec`, `readingCount` anchored to the root `SPEC.md`, the ΔE test), `grammar()` serves the table to the model that writes creatures, `gauntlet*` pick from it. The plan adds `unreleased` with `releasedElements()`, an `element_unreleased` kit rule at the HTTP edge, seeds into a stand database, and a release step that edits frozen lines of `SPEC.md` only with the founder's written ok.
- **Judging is by pixels at 26 m** and by independent judges; the beam's `blue60 > 2000` target is retired (it was measured on the fused sleeve the judges rejected); 1400 at 0.38 s and 1900 at 0.8 s without widening the jacket are the new floor.
- **Deliverables to the founder are pages, not prose**: the comparison page and the stand let him point at a frame; the plan is also published as a page.
