## Reading

A fighter has no ability running for **84%** of its time on the floor. Of that, **60%** is spent with every ability on cooldown — nothing it could do but walk — and **24%** with at least one ability ready and not cast; in **6%** the enemy was inside the reach of a ready targeted shape and the mind still did not fire (waiting for a better angle, line of sight, or its own rule). Both fighters are fully on cooldown at the same moment for **56%** of the match: nobody on the floor can do anything but move.

A median fight lasts **37.2 s** and each fighter casts **11.3** times in it, about **3.1 per 10 s** — one ability every 3.3 s. Cooldown utilisation is **88%**: the fighters cast that share of what the cooldowns would have allowed, so the cadence is set by the cooldowns, not by the minds — shorter cooldowns would translate almost directly into more casts.

Of 558 judged casts, 87% connect, but 140 of the 487 connecting hits were eaten whole by a shield and moved no hp. 5 casts were dodged by a jump or a blink (0.14 per match). 1 of 216 ability slots were never used in their match.

The pair stands more than 12 m apart for 6% of the match and inside 4 m for 42%; mean distance 6.3 m. 36 of 36 fights reach the 30 s burn; 30 of them were ended by the arena's burn rather than by an opponent's hit (the sim logs both as `kill`).

## Method

- Creatures: the top-rated `state='active'`, `kit_active=1` rows of `data/airena.db` with a brain (or `--ids`); every pair among them in ladder order, capped by `--pairs`; each pair plays every seed, sides alternating per seed. Brains are compiled once with `compileBrain` (`src/brain/host.js`) and `reset()` before every match; kits with `compileKit` (`src/skills/compile.js`); bodies with `normalizeBuild(build_json)` (`src/core/config.js`) — every creature in the table has `build_json = NULL`, so all bodies are the default build.
- Tick metrics come from `onFrame` snapshots (`snapshot(world)` in `src/core/sim.js`): `act`, `cd`, position and reported velocity per side, every tick until the bell. Event metrics come from `result.log` (`use`, `damage`, `ignite`, `miss`, `evade`, `refused`, `heal`, `wall`, `shieldBroke`, `burned`, `burnedOut`, `death`, `end`) and from `impact` entries in the frame's `fx` list, which are the only record of a hit that a shield absorbed whole (`damage()` in sim.js returns before logging when nothing got through).
- A cast's outcome is the first of: `impact`/`damage`/`ignite` (hit), `miss` (with its reason), `evade` (i-frames) logged for that fighter and slot between the `use` and the slot's next `use`; a zone with no impact by the bell is an empty zone; self, blink and jump shapes are always "applied" and excluded from hit rates.
- "Ended by": the sim reports `kill` whether the last hp was taken by an opponent or by the arena's burn; this report separates them by matching the loser's `death` line against a `burned` (arena) or `burnedOut` (fire) line on the same tick.
- "Idle" counts only ticks the fighter is alive; the bell tick and the curtain are excluded.

### How much of a fight is standing around waiting for cooldowns

- A fighter has **no ability running for 84 %** of its time alive; for **60 %** it has *every*
  ability on cooldown (wide roster: 84 % / 55 %). Both fighters are fully on cooldown at the same
  instant for **56 %** of the match (wide: 50 %), and both have nothing running for **74 %**.
- Of the 24 % "idle with something ready", only **6 %** has the enemy inside a ready shape's reach
  (wide: 29 % / 9 %). The minds are not sitting on ready abilities; they are waiting for tiles.
- **Cooldown utilisation 88 %** (wide 84 %): fighters cast 88 % of what the cooldowns would ever have
  allowed. Per slot the utilisation is 90–100 % for zones, dashes, beams and self-shields
  (ARRESTER k2 Aura: shield — 6.3 casts per game of a possible 6.5). The cadence is the cooldown table,
  not the mind.
- The cast timeline shows the shape a spectator sees: **5.4 casts in 0–5 s, 0.9 in 5–10 s, 4.2 in
  10–15 s, 2.2 in 15–20 s** — an opening volley, a five-second walk while everything counts down,
  another volley, and so on to the burn. 3.06 casts per fighter per 10 s = one ability every 3.3 s
  *per pair*, most of them arriving in the same half-second.
- Literally standing still is rarer (9 %; STORM/ASH kit-stubs 21–41 %): the bodies walk. What they
  are doing while walking is nothing.

### The arena, not the opponent, ends the fight

- **36 of 36** baseline matches reach the 30 s burn; **34 fighter deaths are the arena's** (30
  matches decided by burn, 2 double-KOs by burn, 4 by a hit). Wide roster: 106 of 132 reach the burn;
  **85 + 4 double** decided by the arena, 41 by a hit, 2 by fire. Every match that ended under 30 s
  was decided by a hit (24) or fire (2); of the 106 that ran longer, 17 were decided by a hit.
- The sim logs all of these as `reason: 'kill'` (`sim.js:2054–2055` after `burn()` marks
  `pendingDeath` at `sim.js:1332`), so the ladder table, the result card and every existing tool see
  "kill" where the crowd saw two bodies wither. `decidedBy` in this report separates them by matching
  the loser's `death` line to a `burned` line on the same tick; the sim should record it itself.
- `config.js:150–162` already says the burn "ratifies a decision already made rather than forcing
  contact" and "the leader at the burn wins 85 %". At 7–16 s cooldowns that is the *normal* ending,
  not the backstop.

### Shields eat the spectacle

- 140 of 487 connecting hits (**29 %**) moved no hp: the shield took all of it. First connected hit
  at 1.5 s, first hp actually lost at 4.7 s (mean).
- `self: shield` costs 7, cools in 6.3 s, and the shield lasts 5 s at 40 hp
  (`registry.js:381`) — ARRESTER and ICEBREAKER keep it up 97 % of the time it could be up, and win
  12-1 / 7-1. `self: shield+heal` (STONE GOLEM·303e, 9.4 s) is the other top kit.
- On the live tree (3 s cooldowns, same 40 × 5 s shield) the absorbed share rose to **38 %** (364 of
  958) and shields broke 198 times in 36 matches. If the shield magnitude/weight does not move with
  the cooldown, the overhaul makes the wall permanent.
- The feed shows nothing for an absorbed hit: `damage()` returns at `sim.js:1258` before any log line,
  and only `shieldBroke` is written (`sim.js:1256`). A watcher sees a cone connect and no number.

### The mortar cannot be aimed, and it shows

- Lob hit rate **48 %** (wide: 110 hit / 117 aim misses of 227 judged) against 81–99 % for every
  other targeted shape: GRAVEDIGGER k2 `Mortar: burn` 45 misses of 88 casts, ASH k3 37 of 70, STORM k3
  35 of 73 — all `aim`. Bolt 81 %, cone 82 %, dash 95 %, beam 98 %, zone 99 %.
- The default landing is the enemy's *current* position (`deliver.js:88 lobLanding`) with a 1.25 s
  flight; the single-number reach argument exists (`sim.js` `startSkill`, "ONE NUMBER — RANGE") but the
  same comment records zero brains using it. The live tree adds an aim point
  (`api.use(name, {x, z})`); it is only a lever once the prompt names it and minds lead the target.

### Metric caveats

- "No act" counts walking as nothing running; it is the spectator's view, not the sim's.
- "In reach" tests distance only (not facing or line of sight) against `range`/`distance` + radii from
  the compiled kit, so it is an upper bound on "could have fired".
- A zone counts as one hit per cast however many ticks it lands; `absorbed` is judged per cast, not per
  tick. 3 casts in the baseline (17 wide) were still in flight at the bell and are `unresolved`.
- All bodies are the default build (see above), so nothing here says anything about the body economy.
- The ladder keeps moving while the server runs; the ids above are the only stable roster. Ratings in
  the Roster table are as of the run.
