## Reading

A fighter has no ability running for **59%** of its time on the floor. Of that, **16%** is spent with every ability on cooldown — nothing it could do but walk — and **42%** with at least one ability ready and not cast; in **13%** the enemy was inside the reach of a ready targeted shape and the mind still did not fire (waiting for a better angle, line of sight, or its own rule). Both fighters are fully on cooldown at the same moment for **16%** of the match: nobody on the floor can do anything but move.

A median fight lasts **16.2 s** and each fighter casts **14.3** times in it, about **8.3 per 10 s** — one ability every 1.2 s. Cooldown utilisation is **65%**: the fighters cast that share of what the cooldowns would have allowed, so the cadence is NOT only the cooldowns: the minds leave a real share of allowed casts on the table, and shorter cooldowns alone will not fill it.

Of 690 judged casts, 88% connect, but 14 of the 608 connecting hits were eaten whole by a shield and moved no hp. 13 casts were dodged — 0 under a leap, 2 through blink i-frames, 11 by stepping across the shot (0.36 per match). 7 of 216 ability slots were never used in their match.

The pair stands more than 12 m apart for 22% of the match and inside 4 m for 21%; mean distance 9.1 m. 3 of 36 fights reach the 30 s burn; 3 of them were ended by the arena's burn rather than by an opponent's hit (the sim logs both as `kill`).

## Method

- Creatures: the top-rated `state='active'`, `kit_active=1` rows of `data/airena.db` with a brain (or `--ids`); every pair among them in ladder order, capped by `--pairs`; each pair plays every seed, sides alternating per seed. Brains are compiled once with `compileBrain` (`src/brain/host.js`) and `reset()` before every match; kits with `compileKit` (`src/skills/compile.js`); bodies with `normalizeBuild(build_json)` (`src/core/config.js`) — every creature in the table has `build_json = NULL`, so all bodies are the default build.
- Tick metrics come from `onFrame` snapshots (`snapshot(world)` in `src/core/sim.js`): `act`, `cd`, position and reported velocity per side, every tick until the bell. Event metrics come from `result.log` (`use`, `damage`, `ignite`, `miss`, `evade`, `refused`, `heal`, `wall`, `shieldBroke`, `burned`, `burnedOut`, `death`, `end`) and from `impact` entries in the frame's `fx` list, which are the only record of a hit that a shield absorbed whole (`damage()` in sim.js returns before logging when nothing got through).
- A cast's outcome is the first of: `impact`/`damage`/`ignite` (hit), `miss` (with its reason), `evade` (i-frames) logged for that fighter and slot between the `use` and the slot's next `use`; a zone with no impact by the bell is an empty zone; self, blink and jump shapes are always "applied" and excluded from hit rates.
- "Ended by": the sim reports `kill` whether the last hp was taken by an opponent or by the arena's burn; this report separates them by matching the loser's `death` line against a `burned` (arena) or `burnedOut` (fire) line on the same tick.
- "Idle" counts only ticks the fighter is alive; the bell tick and the curtain are excluded.
- **Idle+in reach** uses the TRUE centre-to-centre reach, as `src/brain/prompt.js` prints it on the kit card and `tools/checkbehaviour.mjs` verifies it by binary search: beam `own r + 0.2 + range + enemy r + 0.4`, cone `range + enemy r`, bolt `own r + 0.3 + flight + enemy r + 0.35` (flight = range rounded up to whole ticks), mortar `range + splash + enemy r`, field `range + radius + enemy r`, lunge `distance + own r + enemy r`; aura, blink and leap have no hit distance and are not counted. Until 07.09 it read `range + own radius`, which under-counted the reach by 0.4–2.3 m.
- **Dodges** are counted per log line and credited to the body that made them: `miss` with reason `airborne`, an `evade` line (blink i-frames), and a **side-step** — a bolt or mortar `miss` with reason `aim` whose target was crossing the caster→target line faster than 2 m/s at the moment of the miss (the perpendicular component of its velocity, from that tick's own snapshot).
- **Immune** counts a refusal only when the refusing cast carries no damage and no burn atom (read from the caster's compiled kit, or from `SKILLS` for a fixture skill): a control riding a landed damage cast is not a wasted decision. Every `immune` line, filtered or not, is in the raw number beside it.
