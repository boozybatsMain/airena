## Definitions

- **win vs pilots / vs minds / overall** — wins ÷ games (draws count as neither); **score** counts a draw as half. Every pairing is played on both sides of the arena for every seed.
- **hit rate** — connected casts ÷ judged casts (targeted shapes and fields; self, blink and leap are not judged), as in `reports/combat/spectate.mjs`: a connected cast is an `impact` fx with a targeted atom or a `damage`/`ignite` line between the `use` and the slot's next `use`; a `miss` line (with its reason) or an `evade` line is a miss.
- **casts/10 s**, **cd util** (casts ÷ what the cooldowns alone would have allowed), **dead slots** (abilities never used in a match), **mean dist**, **sudden death** (share of matches that ran past the burn), **mean len** — all as spectate.mjs computes them.
- **idle+in reach** — the share of a fighter's ALIVE ticks with no act running while at least one READY ability's TRUE reach already covered the enemy. The reach is centre to centre with BOTH radii in it, exactly as `src/brain/prompt.js` prints it on the kit card and `tools/checkbehaviour.mjs` verifies it by binary search: beam `own r + 0.2 + range + enemy r + 0.4`, cone `range + enemy r`, bolt `own r + 0.3 + flight + enemy r + 0.35` (flight = range rounded up to whole ticks), mortar `range + splash + enemy r`, field `range + radius + enemy r`, lunge `distance + own r + enemy r`; shapes with no hit distance (aura, blink, leap, the free hop) are not counted. Until 07.09 this column used `range + own radius`, which under-counted it by 0.4–2.3 m of reach.
- **dodges/game** — three ways to make one, credited to the body that made it and printed as `total (air + i-frame + side-step)`: a `miss` line with reason `airborne` (the shot passed under a leap), an `evade` line (blink i-frames swallowed it), and a **side-step** — a bolt or mortar `miss` with reason `aim` where the target was crossing the shot line faster than 2 m/s at the moment of the miss (the component of its velocity perpendicular to the caster→target line, from the tick's own snapshot). Counted per log line.
- **immune (raw)** — `immune` log lines whose `by` is this side and whose ability carries NO damage and NO burn atom: a control riding a landed damage cast is not a wasted decision, and the raw count beside it is every `immune` line, filtered or not, so the two definitions can be read against each other. The atoms come from the caster's compiled kit; a fixture skill is read from `SKILLS` in `src/core/config.js` and one with damage counts as damage.
- **refused/game** — `refused` log lines by reason, in the sim's own strings (`startSkill` in `src/core/sim.js`): `cooldown` (the tile had not come back), `silenced`, `busy` (an act was already running), `airborne`. The rarer `stunned` / `dead` / `unknown` stay in `league.json`.
- **telegraph resp.** — of the opponent's wind-up windows (ticks where the opponent's `actPhase` is `windup`, at least 3 ticks long so there was a thought inside it), the share in which this mind turned its velocity by more than 40°, stopped or started moving, or started an ability inside the window. Read it next to dodges: a mind that circles fast registers as responding to long wind-ups whether it noticed them or not. The window count is in brackets in the per-mind table.
- **lead usage** — of this mind's bolt and mortar casts fired while the enemy was moving faster than 2 m/s, the share whose aim was more than 5° off the direct bearing to the enemy at that tick (a bolt flies along the caster's heading, the fx record's `h`; a mortar toward its landing point `x1/z1`). "—" means the kit has no bolt or mortar, or the enemy never moved while one was fired. The cast count is in brackets.
- **faults/game** — thoughts that threw or timed out.

## Method

- Minds: every `<out>/<model>/<creature>.json` with `admitted: true` and a `.js` beside it; the kit and body come from the record (falling back to `/Users/boozybats/Public/Repos/work/Airena/reports/combat/bakeoff/creatures.json`). Brains are compiled once with `compileBrain` and `reset()` before every match; kits with `compileKit`; bodies with `normalizeBuild`.
- (a) every pair of minds, cross-creature and mirror alike, each mind with its own creature's kit and body; (b) every mind against each pilot of the panel holding the SAME kit and body. Each pairing is played on both sides for every seed. Single process, `runMatch` from `src/core/match.js`.
- Tick metrics come from `onFrame` snapshots; event metrics from `result.log` and from `impact`/`bolt`/`lob` entries in the frame's `fx` list.
