## Reading the table

- **c1 and c4 split the panel along one seam.** The two lenses about *place* (design match, interesting) pick c1: the sunken square card on a plaza, the single coral banner, the planet, the tiers in haze — the world of `screen.png`. The three lenses about *discipline* (readability, modern, minimalist) pick c4: one matte material, plain solid boxes read by shadow alone, a nearly empty floor, one accent. Nobody scored either below 60, and the objections to each are the other's strengths.
- **c2 and c3 lose on the floor.** c2's concentric rings and amber arc sit exactly where the game draws its telegraph rings, and a circle contradicts the 40 m walled square; c3's circuit-line inlays, floodlight mast, sun glare and second planet fail every lens at once. Neither contributed more than a detail (c2's planet and plinth stair; c3's flat wall-hung banner and speckled plaster).
- **Consistent drops across all five lenses**: c1's LED strips, plaza tile grid and extra blocks; c2's rings and circle; c3's mast, circuit lines and podium; c4's hollow block tops, pennants on poles, crenellated coping and top-down default view; any second accent hue; any reflective floor; any grid finer than a creature's footprint inside the field; the current viewer's blue rim light on the stone.

## Verdict

One direction, written up in `ARENA-BRIEF.md`: **c1's place with c4's discipline** — the sunken square field with a hairline coping, one plain matte material, exactly the six config blocks as solid boxes, one low warm sun, a floor that carries only the telegraphs, and a far, fogged world (two tier wings, one banner, one planet) kept beyond 2.25·HALF and off the fighting axis.
